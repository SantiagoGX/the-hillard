About the Estate

Where History Hosts
the Living

Built in 1865 by a prosperous Pennsylvania mill owner, The Hillard is a magnificent Second Empire brick mansion in the heart of downtown Wilkes-Barre — fully preserved, beautifully appointed, and available exclusively for your group. No hotel lobbies. No other guests. When you arrive, the whole house is yours.

Whether you're gathering family across generations, celebrating a milestone, hosting a corporate retreat, or planning a wedding weekend — The Hillard becomes the backdrop that makes it unforgettable. Steps from the Susquehanna River Common and downtown Wilkes-Barre's restaurants and galleries.

The Spaces

Every Room
Has a Story

10 bedrooms across three floors, grand communal spaces, and 9 bathrooms — each with its own character and a century of history.

Main Floor

The Grand Parlor

Original parquet flooring, plaster cornice medallions, a crystal chandelier, and a baby grand piano. The room that stops guests in their doorway.

Main Floor

The Formal Dining Room

Twelve-foot ceilings, the original crystal chandelier, and parquet floors that have hosted celebrations for over 150 years. Seats the whole group in style.

Main Floor

The Chef's Kitchen

Viking range, double ovens, Sub-Zero refrigerator, marble island, globe pendant lights, and everything needed to cook for a crowd.

Throughout the House

The Grand Staircase

A sweeping curved staircase with original carved banister, red carpet runner, and wall sconces — the architectural heart of the mansion.

Main Floor

The Music Room

A Ludwig baby grand beneath a crystal chandelier, bookshelves lining the walls, and original plaster molding. Two pianos. One unforgettable room.

Outdoor

The Private Pool

A large heated swim spa and jacuzzi — exclusively yours. Open year-round, lit at night, and set in the private garden behind the mansion.

Upper Floor — Primary Suite

The Heritage Suite

Four-poster canopy bed, original marble fireplace, 12-foot ceilings, and a whirlpool soaking tub en-suite.

Upper Floor

The Rose Room

Soft floral wallpaper, original hardwood floors, and two graceful iron beds. Quiet romance and heirloom charm.

First Floor

The Crimson Suite

Deep crimson walls, marble fireplace, leather sofa, crystal chandelier, and a queen bed. The most dramatic room in the house.

Upper Floor

The Library Room

Original parquet floors, marble fireplace, crystal chandelier, and soaring ceilings. A quiet, elegant retreat with sofa bed.

Upper Floor

The Family Room

Two sets of bunk beds sleeping up to six. Perfect for kids or large groups who want to share the floor together.

Upper Floor

The Double Room

Two double beds, original wallpaper, hardwood floors, and a window overlooking the grounds. Comfortable and quietly elegant.

Third Floor

The Arched Room

A quiet queen room with a distinctive arched window, pendant light, and original hardwood floors. Bright and serene.

Upper Floor

The Garden Room

King bed, warm wood tones, botanical art, and generous natural light. A calm retreat with a connected sitting nook.

Upper Floor

The White Room

Second Floor
